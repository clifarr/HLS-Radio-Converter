A station's name comes from its &name= parameter, so without one the row shows the stream URL. Starved is how long the least-buffered listener has had nothing to play, which is what silence means to a radio. It stays at 0 while audio is flowing; past {{ config["Restart a silent station after"] }} the station is counted as silent, the row turns amber, and the service reports degraded. Upstream idle is different and much less alarming: it counts from the pipeline's side, and a segment is downloaded and decoded in one burst, so it climbs to about one segment and resets - on a station that is playing perfectly.
